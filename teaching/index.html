teaching

Fifteen years of teaching mathematics, from first-year calculus to graduate topics in topological data analysis.

Over fifteen years at Colorado State University, the University of Arizona, and Northern Arizona University, I’ve taught more than 40 university courses across the mathematics curriculum. Teaching is where I built many of the skills I bring to data science:

  • Communicating technical ideas to audiences with very different backgrounds, from first-year biology majors to graduate students.
  • Designing assessments that measure what matters. Mastery-based grading meant defining clear learning objectives and building evaluations around them.
  • Iterating on evidence. Over the last eight years I redesigned my courses around inquiry-based learning and flipped classrooms, adjusting based on what student work showed was and wasn’t working.
  • Mentoring and running programs for students and instructors.

Featured courses

Applied Mathematics for Chemists I & II (Colorado State University). Developed in close collaboration with chemistry faculty, this two-semester sequence teaches a variety of mathematical topics tailored specifically to chemistry applications, in particular physical chemistry. Building it meant working with chemistry faculty to identify the mathematics their students actually needed, then designing the course around those needs. We described the course in the Journal of Chemical Education: Applied Mathematics for Chemistry Majors (2018).

Programs and mentoring

  • Co-organizer, Undergraduate Teaching Assistantship Program, University of Arizona (Fall 2018–Spring 2020). Gave undergraduate math majors experience teaching mathematics: each worked with the instructor of an undergraduate course and met regularly as a group with a mentor.
  • Assistant Director, PACe Center, Colorado State University (January–December 2012). Supervised an undergraduate staff of tutors and proctors for a mixed online pre-calculus program with an on-campus learning and testing center, serving over 1,000 students per semester.
  • Women in STEM Mentorship Project, founding director.
  • Student research mentoring: honors projects in linear algebra and mathematical modeling teams.
